## About Me

I am an undergraduate at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu) studying cognitive science and mathematics. I am currently on leave from university to work as a Forward Deployed Software Engineer at [Palantir Technologies](palantir.com).

In a former life, I was quite involved in speech and debate. If you are a debater looking for resources, feel free to check out my past projects, [CircuitDebater](https://ld.circuitdebater.org/) and [Women in Debate](https://www.windebate.org/). I am currently a coach for the [Harker School](https://www.harker.org/), and I also teach in West Philadelphia schools through the [Netter Center](https://web.sas.upenn.edu/penn-cssp/).

In my spare time, you can find me talking to my cats, doing latte art (poorly), or tinkering with my robot dog. I'm also a big fan of curated [Twitter cat photos](https://x.com/unicouniuni3)!
